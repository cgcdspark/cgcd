## The Bet

## What Happens Next

### S09 · What Happens Next

**Narration**

- `10:09.3` So how does this end? Three ways it could play out.
- `10:13.0` One: business as usual. Nvidia designs its chips years ahead. The roadmap already runs to 2028. A strong new CEO steps in and the machine keeps running. After Steve Jobs, Apple became the first US company worth a trillion dollars.
