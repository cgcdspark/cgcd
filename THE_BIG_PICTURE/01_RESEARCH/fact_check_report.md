## 1. Jensen Huang status: CONFIRMED

The desk's report is **true** and confirmed from primary documents:

- **Nvidia Form 8-K, Item 5.02, filed 12 Nov 2026** ([nvda-20261111.htm](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000082/nvda-20261111.htm)) says: *"On November 11, 2026, the Board of Directors ... accepted the resignation of Jen-Hsun Huang, as NVIDIA's President, Chief Executive Officer and member of the Board, effective immediately."*
- **CFO Colette M. Kress was appointed interim CEO** while the board searches for a permanent CEO. She also stays EVP and CFO.
- **Harvey C. Jones**, a director since 1993, was appointed the board's first-ever Chair.
- Press release Ex. 99.1, "NVIDIA Announces CEO Transition", is dated 12 Nov 2026. The Q3 FY27 results release (17 Nov) quotes Kress as "interim CEO".
- **Caution:** the filing and press release give **no reason** for the resignation. The desk note said it "followed a board investigation". That is **not in any primary document**, so do not narrate it unless the team sources it separately.
- **Narration:** past remarks should be attributed to "Jensen Huang, then Nvidia's CEO" or "Nvidia co-founder and former CEO Jensen Huang".
