## G. Risks (sourced points only)
1. **Taiwan concentration**: TSMC took **72.5% of global foundry revenue** in Q2 2026 (TrendForce, IE) [C38], and 79% of TSMC wafer revenue comes from 7nm and below [C20]. The often-quoted "Taiwan makes 90%+ of the most advanced chips" is UNVERIFIED, so do not use it.
2. **Export controls**: Nvidia took a ~$4.5bn H20 inventory charge in Q1 FY26, and management estimated ~$10.5bn of lost H20 revenue in H1 FY26 [C40]. Every Nvidia outlook from Q4 FY26 onward assumes **no Data Center compute revenue from China** [C10].
3. **Customer concentration**: in Q1 FY27 three direct customers made up **21%, 17% and 16%** of Nvidia's revenue, about 54% combined (10-Q) [C39]. The Q3 FY27 figure (36% for three customers) is UNVERIFIED.
4. **Circular financing**: Nvidia's 2025 investment pact with OpenAI drew "circular financing" criticism (Bernstein's Stacy Rasgon). Jensen Huang rejected the framing; his quoted wording comes from secondary coverage and is UNVERIFIED. Later reports of a ~$250bn guarantee are also UNVERIFIED (AF) [C42].
5. **Depreciation debate**: server useful lives are a management estimate. Meta extended to 5.5 years, while Amazon cut some servers from 6 to 5 years in 2025. Michael Burry estimates ~$176bn of understated depreciation over 2026–28; this is a bear-case estimate (AF, `no`) [C41]. Microsoft moved datacentre buildings to 25-year depreciation this fiscal year (secondary source).
6. **Margin pressure from ramps**: TSMC GM fell 1.3pp in Q3 on the 2nm ramp [C19], and Nvidia guides Q4 GAAP GM down to 71–72% on the Rubin ramp [C10].

## H. Hooks (striking and checkable)
1. **One EUV machine ships in about 40 freight containers, on 20 trucks and three cargo planes** [C32]. DHL attributes the truck and plane figures to ASML. The ~180-tonne weight is single-source and UNVERIFIED.
2. **A single High-NA EUV machine is reported to cost up to ~$400m**, and only one company on Earth makes EUV machines [C29][C30].
3. **Nvidia made $65bn of profit in three months**, about 56 cents of every dollar of revenue [C09][C53].
4. **TSMC takes nearly three of every four dollars spent on contract chipmaking** (72.5%, Q2 2026) [C38].
5. **Four companies plan to spend roughly $700bn+ on capex in 2026**, about three-quarters more than in 2025 [C52]. Alphabet and Meta ran negative free cash flow in Q3 to pay for it.
